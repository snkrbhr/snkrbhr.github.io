About Me

Advised by Dr. Yamuna Prasad at IIT Jammu, my research targets two coupled bottlenecks in neural network compression: calibration-domain bias — pruning methods that depend on training data introduce privacy risk and distribution-shift vulnerability — and memory overhead of coupled pruning pipelines, where jointly scoring and pruning requires storing full-resolution activations, becoming infeasible at LLM scale. I show that intrinsic weight geometry (low-rank residual structure, spectral properties, latent manifold deviation) encodes sufficient information to identify redundant parameters without any calibration data. My work spans CNNs to 70B-parameter LLMs, unstructured to structured pruning, and extends to robustness in safety-critical medical imaging. Works published at EMNLP 2026, TMLR 2026, IJCNN 2025, and IEEE TCE 2026; under review at ICLR 2027.
